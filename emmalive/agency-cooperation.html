06 · HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about joining and managing an Emma Agency.

1 What is an Emma Agent?
An Emma Agent focuses on recruiting and supporting Hosts. Instead of participating as an individual Host, an Agent can invite Hosts, mentor a team, monitor activity, and review commissions or bonuses.
2 How do I register an Emma Agency?
Open the current Emma Agency registration link and create your Agency account using the requested information.
3 How do Emma Agents make money?
Emma Agent earnings can come from several sources, including commission on Host activity, Host recruitment rewards, weekly bonuses, and Sub-Agents. Earnings depend on eligibility and current program rules.
4 What is an Emma Sub-Agent?
A Sub-Agent is an additional recruiter or team builder connected to a larger Emma Agency structure. Sub-Agents can help bring new eligible Hosts into the network and support recruitment growth.
5 What if I have questions or problems regarding Emma Live agency?
Feel free to contact our team via WhatsApp and we will help with your Agency questions.
