You can join as a user, become an Emma Host and participate directly, or build an Emma Agency to recruit and support a team of Hosts.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Emma Live, Host participation and Agency registration.

1What is Emma Live?
Emma Live is a live video chat platform where adults can meet new people, join real-time conversations, and connect with users from different countries. The platform also offers participation opportunities for eligible Hosts and Agencies.
2Is Emma Live safe?
Emma uses account and face-verification measures designed to help reduce fake identities and automated accounts. Like any social platform, users should also protect their personal information, communicate responsibly, and follow Emma's current community guidelines.
3What is the difference between an Emma Host and an Emma Agency?
An Emma Host participates directly in the platform by interacting with users through eligible live features. An Emma Agency works more on the management side by recruiting, organizing, and supporting a team of Hosts. The two roles have different responsibilities and earning structures.
4Can Emma Hosts earn money?
Emma Hosts may earn through qualifying activities such as calls, virtual gifts, messages, tasks, or other Host programs available on their account. Earnings are not guaranteed. Available features, requirements, conversion rates, bonuses, and withdrawal rules can vary depending on the account, market, and current Emma policies.
5How do I become an Emma Agency?
Start through the current Emma Agency registration process via this agency link and create your Agency account using the required information. Once your account is set up, you can access the available Agency tools and begin following the current requirements for recruiting and managing eligible Hosts. For a full step-by-step explanation, visit the Emma Agency page.
