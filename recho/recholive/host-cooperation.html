07 / Live standards

Host Live Streaming Requirements

Follow these requirements to maintain a safe streaming environment and remain eligible for host rewards and bonuses.

  • Use bright lighting during live streams
  • Use a stable internet connection
  • Avoid lying down during the stream
  • Keep the live environment clean and appropriate
  • Greet users when they enter the live room
  • Thank users when they send gifts
  • Respond to incoming video calls when required
  • Keep your face visible in the camera view
  • Interact actively with viewers

If a Host repeatedly misses incoming video calls, salary may be canceled and the Host may be removed from the platform. Low performance or failure to meet required standards may also affect salary or rewards.
