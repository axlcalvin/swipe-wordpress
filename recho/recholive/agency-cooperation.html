01 / Overview

What is Recho Live Agent

As a Recho Live agency, you build your own team by recruiting hosts and sub-agents with official platform support through a free registration process. You'll guide your team, monitor their performance, and help them complete tasks to maximize their growth. As an agent, you earn a transparent percentage-based commission from your team's revenue, giving you the opportunity to grow your livestreaming business within a global livestreaming community.

Agent Responsibility:

  • Recruit New Hosts and Sub-Agents with Invitation Link
  • Guide New Team Members
  • Monitor Team Activity & Performance
  • Help Hosts Complete Tasks & Programs
  • Earn Percentage-Based Commission
