How to Invite Host

  1. Log in to your agent account on the Recho Live app.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Tap “Agent.”
  4. Select “Invite Host.”
  5. Tap “Copy Invitation Link” and share it with the host you want to invite.

07 / RECHO AGENT

How to Invite Sub-Agent

  1. Log in to your agent account on the Recho Live app.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Tap “Agent.”
  4. Select “Invite Sub-Agent.”
  5. Tap “Copy Invitation Link” and share it with the sub-agent you want to invite.

How to Withdraw Earnings

  1. Log in to Recho Live using your agent or host account.
  2. Open your Profile, then tap “My Coin.”
  3. Tap “Withdraw.”
  4. Select “Bind Phone Number.”
  5. Tap “Go Bind,” then enter the verification code sent to your WhatsApp.
  6. Select “Bind Epay Account.”
  7. Enter your Epay account using a valid email address, then confirm.

12 / RECHO AGENT

FAQ

Find the answers to most common questions about the agency commission system, and before you take your first step as an official Recho Live agency.

How much commission can I earn as an agency?

Your commission potential is equal to your team’s earning, with a minimum ratio of 4% and maximum of 30% of their earning. Additionally, Recho Live also have maximum $500 daily withdrawal limit.

What are my main responsibilities as a Recho Live agent?

As an official Recho Live agency, your primary role is to invite, mentor, and manage a team of hosts and sub-agents. You will recruit new talent, guide them to increase their income, and oversee your team using the app's management dashboard.

Can I recruit hosts or sub-agents who are already in another agency?

No. Platform rules require hosts or sub-agents to officially leave and be released by their current agency before they can accept your invitation. This rule also applies to your hosts and sub-agents.

What details are required to set up my official agency account?

You only need a verified phone number to create your agency and start recruiting immediately. Furthermore, to withdraw your agency commission, you need to connect a verified Epay account with your Recho Live account.

Is My Epay Account Information Safe?

Yes. Recho agency commission system only requires basic Epay information such as email and name. Recho Live does not require your password, pin, or any other private information.

Who do I contact if I experience technical issues or account problems?

Please reach out to our support team. As an official Recho Live agency, we act as your direct connection and will work with Recho Live admin to get your issues resolved through the official channel.

Your account information, hosts’ or sub-agents’ data, and payment information are private and are only disclosed to resolve your issues.
