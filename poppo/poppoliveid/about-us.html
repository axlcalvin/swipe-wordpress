✦ OUR STORY

Who We Are

Connecting creators, agency partners, and new opportunities.

Poppo Live is a popular social live-streaming app. It is built to create a fun and interactive space for users all over the world.

Since starting in 2022, Poppo Live has grown to more than 180 countries, becoming very popular in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and Latin America. Today, the app has over 10 million downloads, proving that users trust it and enjoy using it daily.

We focus on helping people: The Agency Partners who want to build a real business, and the Hosts who want to earn money from their talents. With Poppo Live Agency, everyone has a chance to succeed in the creator economy.
