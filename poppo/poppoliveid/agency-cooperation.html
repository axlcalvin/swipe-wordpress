How Much Commission Do You Earn?

Your commission percentage goes up as your team earns more. It is based on your team's total points over the last 30 days.

Tier Team's 30-Day Earnings Live/Party Bonus Chat Bonus
D 0 ~ 2M Points 4% 4%
C 2M ~ 10M Points 8% 8%
B 10M ~ 50M Points 12% 12%
A 50M ~ 150M Points 16% 16%
S 150M+ Points 20% 20%
  • Your tier is calculated based on the last 30 days of income.
  • Your tier can go up or down every month based on your team's activity.

Agency Face Verification

All agency owners must verify their identity to prevent fraud and unlock withdrawals.

  1. 01

    Go to Profile

    Log in to your agency account and go to Profile.

  2. 02

    Face Verification Menu

    Tap the Face Verification security menu.

  3. 03

    Start Authentication

    Select the face authentication option.

  1. 04

    Take Photo

    Take a clear photo of your face and click 'start to certificate'.

  2. 05

    Follow Prompts

    Keep your face in the circle. Blink, turn your head, or smile when asked.

  3. 06

    Wait for Approval

    The admin team will check your photo (takes 1-3 business days). Once approved, your agency is fully verified.

How to Withdraw Agency Earnings

Agencies can withdraw their commission once they hit the minimum balance. Need help? Contact our Agency Support Desk.

Supported methods: Epay and bank transfers are the main methods for agencies.

  1. 01

    Go to Profile.

  2. 02

    Tap 'Points'.

  3. 03

    Tap 'Withdraw Now'.

  4. 04

    Add your payment method.

  5. 05

    Choose Epay, USDT, etc.

  6. 06

    Create a Transaction PIN.

  7. 07

    Link your email.

  8. 08

    Go back to 'Points' and tap 'Withdraw Now'.

  9. 09

    Enter the amount and confirm. Arrives in about 72 hours.

✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for new agency partners.

How much money can an agency make?+

Your income depends on your team's size and activity. Top agencies earn a 20% commission on millions of points every month.

What is my main job as an agency?+

Finding good streamers, teaching them how to earn more, and solving their account problems.

Can I take a host from another agency?+

No. A host must leave their old agency first before you can invite them.

What do I need to start an agency?+

You just need an agency invitation code from us to register your account.

Is my bank data safe?+

Yes. The system only needs your basic e-wallet or bank name. It never asks for your passwords or PINs.

Who do I call if my team has technical issues?+

Contact our Agency Support Desk. We talk directly to the app developers to fix your problems fast.
