✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Agent questions, answered

Registration, commission tiers, withdrawals, and growing your agency.

How do I register as a Poppo Live agent? +

Download the Poppo Live app, create an account, and copy your Poppo ID from your profile. Then click the agency registration link, paste your ID, and enter the verification code sent to your app. Your agency will be activated within 1-3 business days.

What is the commission structure for agents? +

Agents earn 4% to 20% commission based on total points generated by hosts in the last 30 days. Level D (0-2M points) earns 4%, Level C (2-10M) earns 8%, Level B (10-50M) earns 12%, Level A (50-150M) earns 16%, and Level S (150M+) earns 20%.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount for agents? +

The minimum withdrawal amount is $10 (equivalent to 100,000 points). Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours to your chosen payment method.

Can I invite other agents to join under me? +

Yes! You can invite sub-agents through the Agent Center menu. Copy your unique invitation link and share it with potential sub-agents. Build your network and expand your earning potential.

How do I add hosts to my agency? +

Go to your profile, click ‘Agent’ menu, select ‘Add Host’, and enter the host’s ID and invitation code. The host will receive an invitation message and must accept it to join your agency.
