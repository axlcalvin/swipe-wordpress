Everything you need to know before joining Poppo Live as an agent or host.

Poppo Live is a global livestream social platform launched in 2022, connecting over 10 million users across 180 countries. It allows hosts to earn through live streaming and agents to earn commission by managing host networks.

Agents earn between 4% to 20% commission based on monthly points generated by their hosts. Level D (0-2M points) earns 4%, while Level S (150M+ points) earns 20%. Commission applies to both LIVE/PARTY and Chat activities.

Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours with a minimum payout of $10. You can withdraw points earned as either an agent or host by setting up a payment method in the app and creating a secure payout password.

Host face verification typically takes 1-3 business days. Once approved, you can start streaming immediately and begin earning from viewer gifts and interactions.
