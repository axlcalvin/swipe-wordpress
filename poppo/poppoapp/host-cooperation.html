Face Verification Process

Follow all 5 steps below in the app after registration. Verification takes 1-3 business days.

  1. 1

    Open Live mode

    Click the ‘Live’ button on the homepage to begin.

  2. 2

    Upload your photo

    Upload a clear, well-lit photo of yourself.

  3. 3

    Start certification

    Click ‘Start to Certificate’ to launch the verification flow.

  4. 4

    Position your face in the frame

    Follow on-screen instructions: blink, turn your head, and smile.

  5. 5

Verification tips

  • Use good lighting
  • Remove glasses, hats, and masks
  • Use your front camera
  • Keep your face clearly visible

✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Host questions, answered

Registration, earning, verification, and joining an agency.

How do I register as a Poppo Live host? +

Download the Poppo Live app, create an account with your details, and use Referral ID 4345306. After registration, go to ‘My Agency’ in your profile, enter your agency ID, and complete face verification. Approval takes 1-3 business days.

How do hosts earn money on Poppo Live? +

Hosts earn points through virtual gifts from viewers during live streams, participation in PK battles, party rooms, and private calls/chats. Points can be withdrawn as cash (10,000 points = $1) with a minimum withdrawal of $10.

What is face verification and how long does it take? +

Face verification is a security process to confirm your identity. You’ll need to upload a clear photo and follow on-screen instructions (blink, turn head, etc.). Verification typically takes 1-3 business days. Make sure you’re in good lighting and remove glasses, hats, or masks.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount for hosts? +

The minimum withdrawal amount is $10 (approximately 100,000 points). Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours to your chosen payment method.

Can I join an agency after creating my account? +

Yes! Go to your profile, click ‘My Agency’, enter the agency ID that invited you, and click ‘Submit’. You must be verified as a host before you can officially join an agency.
