A global livestream platform since 2022

Poppo Live is a trend-setting video live social app committed to building a vibrant, interactive entertainment platform for users worldwide. Since launching in 2022, we have expanded across more than 180 countries with an experience built for connection, discovery, and live entertainment.

We have grown rapidly across Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Africa, and Latin America, surpassing 20 million downloads and earning the trust of a global community of hosts and viewers.

Whether you love discovering live content or dream of sharing your talent on stage, Poppo Live gives everyone a place to belong — and a platform to turn passion into opportunity.
