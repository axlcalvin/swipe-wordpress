Poppo Live Agent Requirements

Before you begin agency registration, make sure you meet these requirements. All details below are based on the current Poppo Live agent onboarding process.

  • A valid Poppo Live account with your Poppo ID ready for the registration form.
  • Access to the official agency registration link and an agency invitation code. Contact us if you need assistance obtaining a code.
  • Ability to receive the in-app verification message during registration.
  • Completion of face verification for your agent account after registration.
  • Commitment to platform rules — including not recruiting hosts already in another agency, minors, or anyone with falsified information.

How to Set Face Verification for Agent

Face verification validates your identity within the system. See our face verification guide for additional context.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  1. 4

  3. 6

    Wait for approval

    Poppo Live will review your submission, usually within 1–3 working days. Once approved, your account is verified as an agent.

How Poppo Agency Commission Works

Agent commission is tied to your agency's recent performance. Rates apply to LIVE/PARTY activity and Chat activity separately, calculated from the last 30 days of team income.

How Poppo Agency Earnings Work

Agency earnings follow a clear chain: host activity drives agency performance, which determines your commission level and unlocks additional rewards. Results depend on team effort — there are no fixed income guarantees.

Host activity — When hosts under your agency go live, host parties, or chat, they generate points. This activity is the foundation of agency performance.

Agency performance — Combined team points over the last 30 days set your agency level (D through S) and corresponding commission rate.

Commission & rewards — You earn a percentage of team income at your current rate, plus optional bonuses from inviting hosts, inviting sub-agents, and weekly agent rankings.

Poppo Agency Rules and Responsibilities

Poppo Live Agents are responsible for managing hosts and sub-agents, maintaining activity, and complying with platform regulations. The following actions are prohibited.

  • Recruiting host who's already in an agency to join their own agency
  • Recruiting minor/underage hosts
  • Faking host's personal information

Poppo Agent Withdrawal

Agents can withdraw earnings after meeting the minimum balance requirement. For troubleshooting and regional details, see our Poppo Live withdrawal guide.

Poppo Live mainly supports Epay and bank transfer withdrawal methods. However, the availability may vary in each region.

  1. 1

    Head to Your Profile

    Open Poppo Live and navigate to your profile page.

  2. 2

    Open the Points Menu

    Tap Points in your profile to access your earnings overview.

  3. 3

    Press Withdraw Now

    Your available balance will be shown here. Tap Withdraw Now to proceed.

  4. 4

    Add Your Payment Method

    If you're withdrawing for the first time, tap Add a Payment Method to register one before continuing.

  5. 5

    Select a Payment Method

    Poppo Live supports Epay, USDT, PayPal, bank transfer, and e-wallet. Availability depends on your location.

  6. 6

    Create a Transaction PIN

    Set up a secure PIN that will be required each time you withdraw. Avoid predictable combinations to keep your funds safe.

  7. 7

    Link Your Payout Email

    Enter the email connected to your payment account to bind it to your Poppo Live profile.

  8. 8

    Return to Points and Tap Withdraw Now

    Now that your payment method is in place, go back to the Points menu and tap Withdraw Now again.

  9. 9

    Enter Your Amount and Submit

    Fill in your desired withdrawal amount and confirm. Funds are typically delivered within 72 hours.
