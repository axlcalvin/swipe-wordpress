Who We Are

Poppo Live is a trend-setting video live social app, committed to building a vibrant and interactive entertainment platform for global users.

Since launching in 2022, Poppo Live has expanded across more than 180 countries, with strong growth in key markets such as Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and Latin America. To date, the platform has reached over 10+ million downloads, reflecting consistent trust and engagement from users around the world.

We are built for everyone who loves connection, fresh experiences, and entertainment — from audiences looking for engaging live content, to Hosts and Agents ready to turn their talent and network into real opportunity. At Poppo Live, everyone can find their own stage and be part of a growing live streaming story.
